Group Tour · 7 Days

7-Day Morocco Highlights Group Tour

Casablanca → Marrakech → Sahara Desert → Fes → Chefchaouen

Trip Overview

Morocco's greatest hits, one shared loop

Our signature route covers everything first-time visitors want to see — imperial cities, the High Atlas, a night in the Sahara, and the blue-washed lanes of Chefchaouen — split across a full week and shared with a small group of fellow travelers.

Because the vehicle, guide, and most accommodation are shared, this loop covers far more ground per euro than booking each leg privately.

📍 Casablanca to Fes👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Departs every Sunday

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Arrive

Casablanca to Marrakech

Group pickup in Casablanca with a short stop at the Hassan II Mosque, then on to Marrakech for the evening — free time to explore Jemaa el-Fnaa.

Day 2Marrakech

Marrakech city exploration

Guided walk through the medina, souks, Bahia Palace, and Majorelle Garden. Evening free to explore the food stalls of Jemaa el-Fnaa with the group.

Day 3Atlas

Marrakech to Dades Valley via the High Atlas

Cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass, stop at the Ait Benhaddou kasbah, and continue through the Valley of Roses to the Dades Valley.

Day 4Desert

Dades Valley to Merzouga Sahara camp

Morning at the Dades Gorge, then across the Todra Gorge to Merzouga. Camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night at the shared desert camp.

Day 5Transfer

Merzouga to Fes via the Ziz Valley

Sunrise over the dunes, then a scenic drive north through the Ziz Valley and Middle Atlas cedar forests, arriving in Fes by evening.

Day 6Fes

Fes medina and Chefchaouen day trip

Morning guided walk through the Fes el-Bali medina and tanneries, then an optional day trip to the blue-washed streets of Chefchaouen.

Day 7Depart

Departure from Fes

Free morning before your group transfer to Fes airport or train station. Trip ends.

FAQs

7-Day Highlights Tour Questions

Can I join or leave the trip partway through?

Yes, within reason — let us know at booking if you'd like to join in Marrakech instead of Casablanca, or finish elsewhere. Pricing may be adjusted accordingly.

Is the Chefchaouen day trip mandatory?

No — it's offered as an optional add-on for travelers who want to see the blue city. Those who skip it have a free day in Fes instead.

How much walking is involved?

Guided city walks in Marrakech and Fes cover a few kilometers over uneven medina streets. The camel trek is a gentle, non-strenuous ride.

Is accommodation shared for all 6 nights?

Yes, the €499 price is based on shared twin/dorm rooms throughout. A private room upgrade is available on request, subject to availability.

What's the group size like on this route?

Groups typically run 8–16 travelers — a mix of solo travelers, couples, and friends, mostly in their 20s–40s.
