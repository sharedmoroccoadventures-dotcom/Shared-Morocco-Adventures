About This Trip

Marrakech's medina, on foot with a local guide

No minivan, no long drive — just a local guide on foot and a small shared group exploring Marrakech's UNESCO-listed medina. This is our cheapest and shortest trip, ideal for your first morning in the city or a low-cost half-day between longer excursions.

The route winds from the chaos of Jemaa el-Fnaa square through the covered souks (spices, dyers, leather, lanterns), past the 19th-century Bahia Palace, the iconic Koutoubia Mosque minaret, and the beautifully restored Saadian Tombs.

FAQs

Walking Tour Questions

How much walking is involved?

Around 3 km at an easy pace over 2.5–3 hours, with regular stops. Comfortable, closed-toe walking shoes are recommended for the uneven medina lanes.

Is this suitable for children or older travelers?

Yes — the pace is relaxed with plenty of stops, though the medina can be crowded and streets are cobbled/uneven in places.

Can I combine this with another day trip?

Yes — many travelers do this walking tour on arrival day, then a longer trip like Agafay Desert or Ourika Valley later in their stay.

Do I need to pay any entrance fees on the day?

Yes, a small combined fee (~€7) for the Bahia Palace and Saadian Tombs is payable on-site in cash — this isn't included in the trip price.
