Group Tour · 6 Days

6-Day Morocco Imperial Cities & Sahara Escape

Marrakech → Ait Benhaddou → Dades → Todra → Merzouga → Marrakech

Trip Overview

From Marrakech to the desert on a relaxed shared route

This route blends Morocco’s most photogenic landscapes with the best of a classic shared-group journey: dramatic mountain passes, kasbahs, lush valleys and a memorable night in the Sahara. It is perfect for travelers who want a rich experience without the complexity of planning every leg themselves.

The pace is steady and social, with comfortable stops, easy logistics and plenty of time for photos, scenic walks and a real desert experience under the stars.

📍 Round-trip from Marrakech🚗 About 1,000 km total👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Fixed departures

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Arrival

Arrival in Marrakech

Arrival day. Meet your guide and transfer to your base in Marrakech. Use the evening to settle in, enjoy a Moroccan dinner and explore the lively squares, souks and rooftop terraces of the medina.

Day 2Atlas

Marrakech to Ait Benhaddou & Dades Valley

≈260 km · around 4 hrs driving. Cross the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass and stop at the UNESCO-listed Ait Benhaddou kasbah. Continue through the Valley of Roses and onward to the Dades Valley for a relaxed evening in the gorge country.

Day 3Gorges

Dades Valley to Todra Gorge and Merzouga

≈230 km total · about 4.5 hrs driving. Travel through dramatic canyons and Berber villages, with time for a walk at the Todra Gorge before reaching the edge of the Sahara near Merzouga. The afternoon ends with a sunset camel ride into the dunes.

Day 4Desert

Merzouga desert camp & sunrise experience

Spend a full day in the Erg Chebbi dunes. Sunrise on the dunes, free time to explore the area, local music and dinner around the campfire, and a night under the stars in a shared desert camp. This is the highlight for many travelers on this route.

Day 5Return

Merzouga to Ouarzazate via the Atlas

≈330 km · roughly 5 hrs driving. Leave the desert behind and drive back across the plains and the High Atlas. Stop in Ouarzazate for a final night before the scenic return toward Marrakech.

Day 6Depart

Ouarzazate back to Marrakech

≈210 km · around 3.5 hrs driving. Cross back through the mountain pass and arrive in Marrakech by mid-afternoon. End of the trip and airport or accommodation transfer as needed.
