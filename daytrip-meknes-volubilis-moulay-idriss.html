About This Trip

Three sites, one great-value day

The single most popular history day trip from Fes and our best-value route — three sites in one shared group day: the imperial city of Meknes, the exceptionally preserved Roman ruins at Volubilis, and the hillside holy town of Moulay Idriss Zerhoun.

Because all three stops sit close together, this is also our shortest full-day route from Fes — a lower price for a route packed with history, including Volubilis's remarkably intact 2,000-year-old floor mosaics.

FAQs

Meknes & Volubilis Questions

Is Volubilis worth the entrance fee?

Yes — it's one of Morocco's best-preserved Roman sites, with mosaics still visible in situ. The stop is roughly 45 minutes to an hour.

Can non-Muslims enter Moulay Idriss?

Yes, the town itself is open to all visitors — only the shrine's inner sanctuary is restricted to Muslims. The hillside views are the highlight for most travelers.

Is this a good first day trip from Fes?

Yes — it's our shortest full-day route, making it an easy, low-cost introduction to day trips before a longer one like Chefchaouen.
