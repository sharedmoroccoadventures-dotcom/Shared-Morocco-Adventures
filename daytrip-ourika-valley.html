About This Trip

The easiest mountain escape from Marrakech

The easiest budget day trip from Marrakech: a short hop into the High Atlas foothills for mountain air, Berber villages, and a waterfall hike, all in about 7 hours round trip including stops.

Stops include a women's argan oil cooperative, a scenic drive through terraced Berber villages along the Ourika river, and a guided walk toward the Setti Fatma waterfalls, with free time for lunch by the river.

📍 From Marrakech🚗 ≈130 km round trip · about 3.5 hrs driving🗓 Departs daily

Good to Know

A gentle first taste of the Atlas

The waterfall hike involves some uneven, rocky paths — trainers or light hiking shoes are recommended. A guided option all the way to the falls is available for a small local fee.

Booking terms: Send an enquiry first; we confirm the departure time, final price, and pickup details by email. No payment is taken through this website.

FAQs

Ourika Valley Questions

How difficult is the waterfall walk?

Easy to moderate — a well-trodden path with some rocky, uneven sections. Most fitness levels can manage it at a relaxed pace.

Is lunch included?

No, but there are riverside cafés near Setti Fatma serving tagines and grilled dishes, generally €5–10 per person.

Can I combine this with the Agafay Desert trip?

Yes — many travelers do Ourika Valley one day and Agafay Desert the next, since both depart from Marrakech.
