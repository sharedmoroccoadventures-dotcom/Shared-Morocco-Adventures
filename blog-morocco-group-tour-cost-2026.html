How Much Does a Morocco Group Tour Cost in 2026?

A real breakdown of what our tours cost, what's included, and exactly what moves the price up or down.

"How much does a Morocco tour cost" is a hard question to answer honestly online, because so many listings quote a headline price and leave out what actually gets added later. Here's a straight breakdown of what our own group tours cost as of 2026, what's already included in that price, and the handful of things that actually move it up or down.

Our Full Price Range: €135 to €830

Across our 11 fixed-departure tours, prices run from €135 per person up to €830, and the gap between them comes down to three things: how many days and nights you're booking, how far the route travels, and whether any night on the trip is upgraded from shared accommodation to something more comfortable. Here's every tour, shortest to longest:

  • 5-Day Shared Marrakech Adventure Tour — from €135. Based in one Marrakech hostel, with daily round-trip excursions rather than changing accommodation each night.
  • 3-Day Atlas Mountains & Agafay Group Escape — from €219. A short Marrakech-based escape into the mountains and the Agafay stone desert.
  • 4-Day Sahara Desert Group Tour — from €249. Our best-selling route: Marrakech to Merzouga and back, camel trek and desert camp included.
  • 4-Day Fes, Chefchaouen & Volubilis Tour — from €289. A round trip from Fes covering the Roman ruins at Volubilis and the blue city of Chefchaouen.
  • 5-Day Marrakech, Atlas & Sahara Adventure — from €329. The same desert route as the 4-day tour, spread over an extra day at a slower pace.
  • 8-Day Marrakech Adventure & Sahara Desert Tour — from €359. Bundles the 5-day Marrakech excursion loop with a 3-day Sahara loop, priced below booking both separately.
  • 6-Day Marrakech, Sahara Desert & Fes Tour — from €412. The one-way version of our Sahara route, finishing in Fes with a day of guided sightseeing instead of returning to Marrakech.
  • 7-Day Morocco Highlights Group Tour — from €499. A wider loop through Marrakech, the Sahara, Fes and Chefchaouen.
  • 8-Day Morocco Desert & Atlantic Coast Tour — from €579. Combines the desert loop with a stop on the Atlantic coast at Essaouira.
  • 10-Day Morocco Grand Circuit Group Tour — from €749. Casablanca to Marrakech, covering the imperial cities, the Sahara, and the High Atlas without rushing.
  • 8-Day Marrakech, Sahara, Fes & Chefchaouen Tour — from €830. The longest Sahara-to-Fes route, and currently our only tour built around a private, luxury desert camp instead of a shared one — which is what sets its price apart from the otherwise similarly-priced 7-day and 10-day routes.

What Actually Moves the Price

Duration is the obvious driver — more nights and more driving days cost more to run — but three other things matter just as much once you're comparing similarly-sized trips:

Half board vs. room only. Nights that include dinner as well as breakfast (typically the Dades Gorges and desert-camp nights) cost more than a simple room-only night, but they also save you from finding and paying for dinner separately in places with fewer options.

Shared vs. luxury desert camp. A shared Berber-style camp with dorm-style tents is the standard on most of our desert routes. A handful of nights are upgraded to a private, luxury camp with proper beds and an en-suite tent — that single change is the main reason our 8-Day Marrakech, Sahara, Fes & Chefchaouen Tour costs noticeably more than routes of a similar length.

One-way vs. round-trip. Tours that end in a different city than they start (like our 6-day and 8-day Fes routes) sometimes cost more per day than a round trip covering similar ground, since the logistics don't loop back to a single base.

What's Included in the Price

Every tour we run includes, at minimum: shared transport in an air-conditioned minivan, an English/French-speaking driver-guide, the accommodation for every night of the trip, and breakfast daily. Most routes also include at least one or two dinners, and any camel trek or desert-camp night is fully covered. Check each tour's own page for its exact list — the specifics (how many dinners, which nights are half board) vary by route.

What's Not Included

Consistently excluded across every tour: flights and travel insurance, lunches and any dinners not specifically listed as included, entrance fees to optional sites, and tips for your driver and guide. If you'd rather not share a room, a private upgrade is available on request on every tour, subject to availability, for an additional cost on top of the listed price.

Why Shared Pricing Beats a Private Itinerary

All of the prices above are per person, based on a shared vehicle and shared accommodation — that's what keeps them where they are.
