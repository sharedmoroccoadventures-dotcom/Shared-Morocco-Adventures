4-Day Sahara Desert Group Tour

Marrakech → Ait Benhaddou → Dades Gorge → Todra Gorge → Merzouga Desert Camp → Marrakech

Trip Overview

Cross the Atlas to a night in the dunes

This is our most-booked route: a fixed-departure group trip that takes you from Marrakech, over the High Atlas passes, through Berber valleys and dramatic gorges, and into the Sahara for a camel trek and overnight desert camp — all shared with a small group of fellow travelers.

Transport, driver-guide, and accommodation are shared across the group, which keeps the per-person cost far below a private itinerary covering the same route.

📍 Round-trip from Marrakech👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Departs every Saturday

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Depart

Marrakech to Dades Valley

Early morning group pickup in Marrakech. Cross the High Atlas via Tizi n'Tichka pass, stop at the Ait Benhaddou kasbah (a UNESCO World Heritage site), and continue through the Valley of Roses to an overnight stay in the Dades Valley.

Day 2Gorges

Dades Valley to Merzouga via Todra Gorge

Morning walk through the Dades Gorge's rock formations, then on to the towering Todra Gorge for a short hike. Continue across the desert plains to Merzouga, gateway to the Sahara.

Day 3Desert

Sahara camel trek and desert camp

Late afternoon camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes, arriving at a shared Berber-style desert camp in time for sunset. Dinner around the fire, live music, and a night under the stars.

Day 4Return

Merzouga back to Marrakech

Optional sunrise over the dunes before camels carry the group back to Merzouga. Scenic drive back across the Atlas, with a stop in Ouarzazate, arriving in Marrakech by evening.

Good to Know

Small groups, fixed dates, no surprises

Groups are capped at 16 travelers to keep things sociable without losing the personal touch. Departures run every Saturday year-round — reserve early in peak season (October–April) as routes sell out.

Want your own private room instead of sharing? Let us know when booking — it's available as a paid upgrade, subject to availability.

FAQs

4-Day Sahara Tour Questions

How many people are in the group?

Groups on this route typically run between 6 and 16 travelers, depending on the season.

Is accommodation shared?

Yes — the €249 price is based on a shared twin or dorm-style room. A private room upgrade is available on request for an additional cost.

What should I pack for the desert night?

Desert nights get cold even in summer — bring a warm layer, plus sunscreen, a scarf or hat for wind and sun, and comfortable walking shoes.

Can I join this trip as a solo traveler?

Absolutely — most of our travelers on this route arrive solo. You'll be paired with a same-gender roommate for shared rooms unless you book a private upgrade.

What if the group doesn't fill up?

Unlike many group operators, our departures run on fixed weekly dates regardless of group size — your trip runs even if only a few seats are booked.
