4-Day Sahara Desert Group Tour

Arrival in Marrakech → Ait Benhaddou → Dades Gorges → Merzouga Desert Camp → Marrakech

Trip Overview

Cross the Atlas to a night in the dunes

This is our most-booked route: a fixed-departure group trip that starts with your arrival in Marrakech, then heads over the High Atlas passes, through Berber valleys and dramatic gorges, and into the Sahara for a camel trek and overnight desert camp — all shared with a small group of fellow travelers.

Transport, driver-guide, and accommodation are shared across the group, which keeps the per-person cost far below a private itinerary covering the same route.

📍 Round-trip from Marrakech🚗 ≈1,010 km total👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Departs every Saturday

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Arrive

Arrival in Marrakech

≈6 km · about 15–20 min. Pick-up from Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and transfer to your hostel in the medina. The rest of the day is free to explore Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the surrounding souks — this is also your chance to meet the rest of the group before departure the next morning.

Day 2Dades

Marrakech to Dades Gorges

≈210 km · about 3.5 hrs driving. Early morning group departure from the hostel. Cross the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, stop at the Ait Benhaddou kasbah (a UNESCO World Heritage site), and continue through the Valley of Roses to an overnight stay in the Dades Gorges.

Day 3Desert

Dades Gorges to Merzouga Desert

≈235 km · about 4.5 hrs driving. Drive on via Tinghir and Erfoud to Merzouga, gateway to the Sahara. Late afternoon camel ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes, arriving at your desert camp in time for sunset — dinner around the fire, live music, and a night under the stars.

Day 4Return

Merzouga back to Marrakech

≈560 km total · about 8.5 hrs driving with stops. Optional sunrise over the dunes before camels carry the group back to Merzouga. Scenic drive back across the Atlas, with a stop in Ouarzazate, arriving in Marrakech by evening. Drop-off at your own accommodation in the city — trip ends here.

Where You'll Stay

Accommodation on this route

Three nights, shared throughout.

🕍

Night 1 · Marrakech

Riad Dia, immediately next to Jemaa el-Fnaa — 9.3/10 on Hostelworld, rooftop pool, free breakfast and WiFi. Backup: Hostel Riad Marrakech Rouge, 2 minutes from the square.

🏰

Night 2 · Dades Gorges

Luxury Dades Riad or Auberge Tissadrin, half board (HB) — a kasbah-style riad in the Dades Valley with mudbrick rooms, terrace gorge views, and dinner included.

⛺

Night 3 · Merzouga desert camp

Ideal Desert Camp, half board (HB) — a Berber-style camp at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes with dinner and breakfast included, campfire music, and a night under the stars.

Good to Know

Small groups, fixed dates, no surprises

Groups are capped at 16 travelers to keep things sociable without losing the personal touch. Departures run every Saturday year-round — reserve early in peak season (October–April) as routes sell out.

Day 1 includes airport pick-up and a night at our Marrakech hostel before the group sets off the next morning. On Day 4 you're dropped off at your own accommodation in Marrakech — if you haven't booked anywhere yet, we can drop you at the same hostel instead.

FAQs

4-Day Sahara Tour Questions

How many people are in the group?

Groups on this route typically run between 6 and 16 travelers, depending on the season.

Is accommodation shared?

Yes — the €249 price is based on shared twin/dorm rooms in Marrakech and the Dades Gorges, and a shared tent at the desert camp. A private room upgrade is available on request for an additional cost.

What should I pack for the desert night?

Desert nights get cold even in summer — bring a warm layer, plus sunscreen, a scarf or hat for wind and sun, and comfortable walking shoes.

Can I join this trip as a solo traveler?

Absolutely — most of our travelers on this route arrive solo. You'll be paired with a same-gender roommate for shared rooms unless you book a private upgrade.

Where does the tour pick me up and drop me off?

Day 1 includes airport pick-up and transfer to our Marrakech hostel. On Day 4 you're dropped off in Marrakech at your own accommodation — if you haven't booked anywhere yet, we can drop you at the same hostel instead.

What if the group doesn't fill up?

Unlike many group operators, our departures run on fixed weekly dates regardless of group size — your trip runs even if only a few seats are booked.
