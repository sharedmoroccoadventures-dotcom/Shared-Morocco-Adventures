Chefchaouen Blue City Day Trip

  1. Fes
  2. Rif Mountains
  3. Chefchaouen Medina
  4. Free Time
  5. Fes

≈400 km round trip · about 7 hrs driving

Chefchaouen's blue-washed lanes are the single most-booked day trip from Fes. A shared minivan covers the roughly 3.5-hour drive each way through the green hills of the Rif Mountains, with a full afternoon to wander the town's photogenic streets.

A guided walk covers the winding blue alleyways, the Kasbah Museum, and the main square (Plaza Uta el-Hammam), followed by free time for rooftop cafés and local weaving workshops.

Includes: shared minivan, English/Spanish/French driver-guide, guided medina walk, hotel/riad pickup. Not included: lunch, Kasbah Museum entrance fee, travel insurance.

Meknes, Volubilis & Moulay Idriss Day Trip

  1. Fes
  2. Meknes
  3. Volubilis Roman Ruins
  4. Moulay Idriss Zerhoun
  5. Fes

≈190 km round trip · about 3.5 hrs driving

The single most popular history day trip from Fes and our best-value route — three sites in one shared group day: the imperial city of Meknes, the exceptionally preserved Roman ruins at Volubilis, and the hillside holy town of Moulay Idriss Zerhoun.

Because all three stops sit close together, this is also our shortest full-day route from Fes — a lower price for a route packed with history, including Volubilis's remarkably intact 2,000-year-old floor mosaics.

Bab Mansour GateVolubilis MosaicsMoulay Idriss Zerhoun

Includes: shared minivan, driver-guide, guided stops at all three sites, hotel/riad pickup. Not included: Volubilis entrance fee, lunch, travel insurance.

Ifrane & Middle Atlas Cedar Forest Day Trip

  1. Fes
  2. Ifrane
  3. Azrou Cedar Forest
  4. Barbary Macaques
  5. Fes

≈145 km round trip · about 2.5 hrs driving

Nicknamed the "Switzerland of Morocco," Ifrane is a world away from the Fes medina — sloped roofs, pine-lined streets, and cool mountain air year-round. The route continues into the Middle Atlas cedar forests near Azrou, home to wild Barbary macaques.

A shorter, lower-cost route than our other Fes day trips, and an easy pick for nature lovers or anyone wanting a change of scenery from the imperial cities.

Includes: shared minivan, driver-guide, guided stops in Ifrane and the cedar forest, hotel/riad pickup. Not included: lunch, travel insurance.

Sefrou & Bhalil Half-Day Trip

  1. Fes
  2. Bhalil Cave Houses
  3. Sefrou Old Town
  4. Fes

≈65 km round trip · about 1.5 hrs driving

Our cheapest and most local day trip. Bhalil is known for its troglodyte cave houses, some still inhabited today, while Sefrou is a relaxed former capital famous for cherry orchards, pottery, and a walled medina that feels like a smaller, quieter Fes.

At roughly 3.5 hours round trip, this is a great budget add-on alongside a longer day trip or a few days exploring the Fes medina — it wraps up around lunchtime.

Includes: shared minivan, driver-guide, guided visits to Bhalil and Sefrou, hotel/riad pickup. Not included: lunch, travel insurance.

FAQs

Fes Day Trip Questions

Are these shared or private tours?

All the trips above are shared, small-group departures — you'll travel with other budget travelers in the same minivan, which is what keeps prices low. Private versions are available on request for most routes.

Which day trip should I book first?

Chefchaouen is our most popular, but if you're short on time or budget, Sefrou & Bhalil or Meknes, Volubilis & Moulay Idriss are shorter and cheaper without sacrificing much.

How far in advance should I book Chefchaouen?

It's our most-booked route, so seats can sell out several days ahead in high season (October–April). Booking early is recommended.

Can I combine two day trips in the same visit?

Yes — Sefrou & Bhalil pairs well with a free afternoon in the Fes medina, and many travelers do Meknes/Volubilis on one day and Chefchaouen the next.
