5-Day Shared Marrakech Adventure Tour

Ourika Valley → Agafay Desert → Ouzoud Waterfalls — one Marrakech base, three big excursions

Trip Overview

Marrakech's best nature, no repacking required

This 5-day shared tour is based entirely in Marrakech, with daily round-trip excursions to the Ourika Valley, the Agafay Desert, and the Ouzoud Waterfalls, returning each evening to the same budget hostel in the medina. It's built for travelers who want Marrakech's surrounding nature and adventure activities — Atlas Mountain foothills, a desert camel ride and ATV outing, and Morocco's tallest waterfalls — without switching hotels or repacking a bag each day.

Transport runs in a shared minivan with other small-group travelers, keeping the cost well below a private tour covering the same three excursions.

📍 Based in Marrakech🚗 ≈460–480 km total🛏 4 nights, same hostel🗓 Fixed weekly departures

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Arrive

Arrival in Marrakech

≈6 km · about 15–20 min. Pick-up from Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and private transfer to your hostel in the medina. The rest of the day is free to settle in, rest, and explore Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the surrounding souks at your own pace.

Day 2Ourika

Ourika Valley Excursion

≈84 km round trip · about 2 hrs total driving. Full-day round-trip drive south into the Atlas Mountains foothills to the Ourika Valley. Highlights include Berber villages along the Ourika river, terraced fields, a stop at a working argan oil cooperative, and an optional, weather-dependent short hike to the Setti Fatma waterfalls. Return to Marrakech by early evening.

Day 3Agafay

Agafay Desert: Camel Ride & ATV/Quad Biking

≈70 km round trip · about 1–1.5 hrs total driving. Short drive southwest to the rocky Agafay Desert — Marrakech's closest desert landscape. Activities include a camel ride across the dunes and an ATV/quad biking session through the desert terrain, with views back toward the Atlas Mountains. Return to Marrakech in the afternoon or evening.

Day 4Ouzoud

Ouzoud Waterfalls Excursion

≈300–310 km round trip · about 5–6 hrs total driving. Full-day round trip north to the Ouzoud Waterfalls, the tallest waterfalls in North Africa at around 110 metres. Time to walk the viewpoints, descend to the base of the falls, see the resident Barbary macaque monkeys, and optionally take a small boat ride near the pools. This is the longest driving day of the tour. Return to Marrakech in the evening.

Day 5Depart

Departure

≈6 km · about 15–20 min. Free morning depending on flight time, then private transfer from your hostel to Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) for departure. Trip ends.

FAQs

5-Day Marrakech Adventure Questions

What does this tour cost?

The 5-day shared Marrakech tour is 135 EUR per person, based on shared/group transport and the budget hostel accommodation listed above.

Is this a private or shared tour?

This is a shared/small-group tour — you travel in a minivan with other travelers on each excursion, which keeps the price lower than a private tour. A private version of the same itinerary can be arranged on request.

Do I stay in the same hostel for all 5 days?

Yes. You check in once in Marrakech and return to the same hostel each night after the Ourika, Agafay, and Ouzoud excursions — no repacking or hotel changes.

How far is Ouzoud Waterfalls from Marrakech, and is it a long day?

Ouzoud is about 150–155 km each way (roughly 300 km round trip), with 5–6 hours of driving total, so Day 4 is the longest travel day of the tour. You'll still have several hours at the falls themselves.

Are the camel ride and ATV/quad biking both included on the Agafay Desert day?

Yes, Day 3 includes both a camel ride and an ATV/quad biking session in the Agafay Desert, roughly 35 km from Marrakech.

What should I pack for this tour?

Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a light jacket for the Atlas Mountains (Ourika) and evenings, a swimsuit if you plan to visit the Ouzoud pools, and cash in MAD for entrance fees and tips.

Can this itinerary be customized?

Yes — days can be reordered, extended, or converted to a private tour, and the accommodation can be upgraded from hostel to riad or hotel level.

Is airport transfer included?

Yes, both the Day 1 arrival pick-up and the Day 5 departure transfer to Marrakech Menara Airport are included.
