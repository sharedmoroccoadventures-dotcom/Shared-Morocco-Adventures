Group Tour · 10 Days

10-Day Morocco Grand Circuit Group Tour

Casablanca → Rabat → Fes → Chefchaouen → Sahara Desert → Marrakech

Trip Overview

Morocco's essentials, without rushing

Our longest and most complete route — imperial cities, mountain passes, the blue lanes of Chefchaouen, a night in the Sahara, and Marrakech's medina, all in one shared circuit with enough days built in that no leg feels like a race against the clock.

Total driving distance is around 1,530 km over ten days, the most ground we cover on any single itinerary.

📍 Casablanca to Marrakech👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Departs every other Sunday

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Arrive

Casablanca arrival

Group welcome in Casablanca with a stop at the Hassan II Mosque. Evening free on the seafront corniche. Overnight in Casablanca.

Day 2Rabat

Casablanca to Meknes via Rabat

≈230 km total · about 3 hrs driving. Morning stop in Rabat (≈90 km/1 hr) for the Kasbah of the Udayas and Hassan Tower, then on to Meknes (≈140 km/2 hrs) for the night.

Day 3Volubilis

Meknes to Fes via Volubilis

≈90 km total · about 1.5 hrs driving. Morning tour of Meknes' royal stables, a stop at the Roman ruins of Volubilis (≈30 km/30 min), then on to Fes (≈60 km/1 hr) for the night.

Day 4Fes

Fes medina exploration

Full guided day through the Fes el-Bali medina — tanneries, souks, and the Bou Inania Madrasa. Overnight in Fes.

Day 5Chefchaouen

Fes to Chefchaouen

≈200 km · about 3.5 hrs driving. Scenic drive north through the Rif Mountains. Afternoon free to wander the blue medina, with an optional sunset hike to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint. Overnight in Chefchaouen.

Day 6Transfer

Chefchaouen to Midelt

≈350 km · about 5.5 hrs driving. Long but scenic drive south through the Middle Atlas cedar forests near Ifrane (look out for Barbary macaques) to the mountain gateway town of Midelt. Overnight in Midelt.

Day 7Desert

Midelt to Merzouga Sahara camp

≈120 km · about 2 hrs driving. Short, dramatic drive down through the Ziz Valley gorge to Merzouga. Camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night at the shared desert camp.

Day 8Return

Sahara camp to Ouarzazate

≈330 km · about 5 hrs driving. Optional sunrise over the dunes, then a scenic drive back across the Atlas via Tinghir and the Todra and Dades Gorges, arriving in Ouarzazate for the night.

Day 9Marrakech

Ouarzazate to Marrakech via Ait Benhaddou

≈210 km · about 3.5 hrs driving. Stop at the UNESCO-listed Ait Benhaddou kasbah before crossing the High Atlas to Marrakech, arriving with the evening free in the medina. Overnight in Marrakech.

Day 10Depart

Departure from Marrakech

Free morning before your group transfer to Marrakech airport or onward accommodation. Trip ends.

Where You'll Stay

Accommodation on this route

Nine nights, shared throughout. Expect the same style and price bracket as these real budget properties in each city.

🏙️

Night 1 · Casablanca

A simple city hostel comparable to Aya House or Medina Guesthouse - Hostel — shared or twin rooms, from roughly €15–25/night.

🏛️

Night 2 · Meknes

A small, no-frills hotel near the medina, in the same bracket as Meknes' budget listings from around €14–20/night.

🏨

Nights 3–4 · Fes

A budget medina hostel comparable to Moroccan Dream Hostel or Riad Verus — traditional courtyard house, shared or twin rooms.

💙

Night 5 · Chefchaouen

A small blue-medina guesthouse similar to Bleu Home or Pension Soukia.

🏔️

Night 6 · Midelt

A simple mountain-gateway guesthouse — basic twin rooms, used as an overnight stop before the Ziz Valley descent.

⛺

Night 7 · Merzouga desert camp

Ideal Desert Camp, half board (HB) — dorm tents, shared bathrooms, dinner and breakfast included.

🏰

Night 8 · Ouarzazate

A budget guesthouse near the medina, similar to Le Petit Riad.

🕍

Night 9 · Marrakech

A medina riad comparable to Riad Jennah Rouge or Riad les Oliviers — dorm beds from around €15, near Jemaa el-Fnaa.

FAQs

10-Day Grand Circuit Questions

Is this trip too much driving?

It's our longest route in total distance, but it's spread over ten days with full free days in Fes and rest built into the schedule — no day exceeds about 5.5 hours of driving.

Can I join partway through, e.g. skip the imperial cities?

Yes — let us know at booking if you'd like to join in Fes or Marrakech instead of Casablanca. Pricing is adjusted accordingly.

Is accommodation shared for all 9 nights?

Yes, the €749 price is based on shared twin/dorm rooms throughout. A private room upgrade is available on request, subject to availability.

What should I pack for the desert night?

Desert nights get cold even in summer — bring a warm layer, plus sunscreen, a scarf or hat for wind and sun, and comfortable walking shoes.

What if the group doesn't fill up?

Departures run on fixed dates regardless of group size — your trip runs even if only a few seats are booked.
