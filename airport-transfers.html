Skip the taxi-rank haggling. Fixed prices, a driver waiting with your name at arrivals, and both a budget shared shuttle and a private door-to-door option at both Marrakech Menara (RAK) and Fès–Saïs (FEZ) airports.

FAQs

Airport Transfer Questions

Is the price really fixed?

Yes — you agree the price when you book, with no meter and no surprise add-ons on arrival, unlike most airport taxi ranks.

What if my flight is delayed?

Both shared and private transfers track your flight, so your driver adjusts pickup time automatically at no extra cost.

How much luggage can I bring?

One large suitcase and one carry-on per person is included. Traveling with extra or oversized luggage? Let us know when booking so we can arrange the right vehicle.

Can I book a transfer without booking a tour?

Absolutely — airport transfers are available as a standalone booking, no tour required.

Do you also offer departure transfers?

Yes, both arrival and departure transfers are available at the same fixed prices for Marrakech and Fes.

Landing soon?

Send us your flight details and we'll have a driver waiting.
