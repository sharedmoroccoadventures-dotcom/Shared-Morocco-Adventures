Trip Overview

Marrakech's excursions, plus a night in the dunes

This is our 5-Day Shared Marrakech Adventure Tour extended with a full 3-day Sahara desert loop — the Ourika Valley, the Agafay Desert (camel ride and ATV), and the Ouzoud Waterfalls as round-trip day excursions from Marrakech, followed by a classic Sahara run via the Dades Gorges to a camel trek and desert camp night at Merzouga, then back to Marrakech.

Transport runs in a shared minivan throughout, and accommodation is shared — the same combination that keeps our other tours well below private-tour pricing for the same ground covered.

Day by Day

The journey, day by day

Day1
Day 1 · Arrive≈6 km · about 15–20 min

Arrival in Marrakech

Pick-up from Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and private transfer to your hostel in the medina. The rest of the day is free to explore Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the surrounding souks.

Day2
Day 2 · Ourika≈84 km round trip · about 2 hrs total driving

Ourika Valley Excursion

Round-trip drive into the Atlas Mountains foothills — Berber villages, terraced fields, an argan oil cooperative, and an optional short hike toward the Setti Fatma waterfalls. Return to Marrakech by early evening.

Day3
Day 3 · Agafay≈70 km round trip · about 1–1.5 hrs total driving

Agafay Desert: Camel Ride & ATV/Quad Biking

Short drive to the rocky Agafay Desert for a camel ride and an ATV/quad biking session, with views back toward the Atlas Mountains. Return to Marrakech.

Day4
Day 4 · Ouzoud≈300–310 km round trip · about 5–6 hrs total driving

Ouzoud Waterfalls Excursion

Full-day round trip to Ouzoud, North Africa's tallest waterfall (~110m) — viewpoints, a walk to the base, Barbary macaques, and an optional boat ride. Return to Marrakech in the evening.

Day5
Day 5 · Depart≈210 km · about 3.5 hrs driving

Marrakech → Dades Gorges

Leave Marrakech and cross the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, stopping at the UNESCO-listed Ait Benhaddou kasbah, before continuing to an overnight stay in the Dades Valley. Pack a small overnight bag for this leg — your main luggage stays safely stored at the Marrakech hostel.

Day6
Day 6 · Desert≈235 km total · about 4.5 hrs driving

Dades → Merzouga via Todra Gorge

Morning walk through the Dades Gorge, then on to the towering Todra Gorge (≈55 km/1 hr) for a short hike, continuing to Merzouga (≈180 km/3.5 hrs). Camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night at a shared desert camp — dinner around the fire, live music, and a night under the stars.

Day7
Day 7 · Return≈560 km total · about 8.5 hrs driving with stops

Merzouga → back to Marrakech

Optional sunrise over the dunes before camels carry the group back to Merzouga. Scenic drive back across the Atlas (≈350 km/5.5 hrs), with a stop in Ouarzazate, then a final ≈210 km/3.5 hrs to Marrakech by evening — back to the same hostel for your last night.

Day8
Day 8 · Depart≈6 km · about 15–20 min

Departure

Free morning depending on flight time, then private transfer from your hostel to Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) for departure. Trip ends.

Where You'll Stay

Accommodation on this route

Seven nights, shared throughout. Marrakech nights use the same hostel from the 5-Day Marrakech Adventure Tour.

🕍

Nights 1–4 & 7 · Marrakech

Riad Dia, immediately next to Jemaa el-Fnaa — 9.3/10 on Hostelworld, rooftop pool, free breakfast and WiFi. Backup: Hostel Riad Marrakech Rouge, 2 minutes from the square.

🏰

Night 5 · Dades Gorges

Luxury Dades Riad or Auberge Tissadrin, half board (HB) — a kasbah-style riad with mudbrick rooms and terrace gorge views, dinner included.

⛺

Night 6 · Merzouga desert camp

Ideal Desert Camp, half board (HB) — a Berber-style camp at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes with dinner and breakfast included, campfire music, and a night under the stars.

Confirm current nightly rates and availability directly before booking — hostel pricing shifts seasonally.

FAQs

8-Day Marrakech & Sahara Questions

What does this tour cost?

The 8-day Marrakech Adventure & Sahara Desert tour is 467 EUR per person, based on shared/group transport and shared accommodation throughout.

Is this the same as booking the 5-day tour and the 4-day Sahara tour separately?

It covers the same ground as both, but as one combined itinerary at a bundled price lower than booking each separately, with a single continuous booking rather than two.

Do I need to pack differently for the desert days?

Yes — pack a small overnight bag for Days 5–7 and leave your main luggage securely stored at the Marrakech hostel, which keeps the desert loop light and simple.

How many nights are in the Sahara desert camp?

One night, on Day 6, after the camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes near Merzouga.

Is the return drive from Merzouga to Marrakech a long day?

Yes — at around 560 km and 8.5 hours with stops, Day 7 is the longest single driving day of the tour, broken up by a stop in Ouarzazate.

Can I do the desert loop first and the Marrakech excursions after?

The order can be adjusted on request, subject to availability on both legs — ask us when booking if you'd prefer to start with the Sahara.
