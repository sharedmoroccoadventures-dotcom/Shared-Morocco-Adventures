Booking

Reservations & Payment

How do I book a spot?

Pick a trip from our Group Tours page and reach out through the contact form with your preferred departure date. We'll confirm availability and send payment instructions.

Do I need to pay the full amount upfront?

No — a deposit secures your seat, with the balance due before departure. Full details are sent with your booking confirmation.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are refundable up to 14 days before departure. Cancellations within 14 days are handled case-by-case — contact us and we'll do our best to help.

On The Road

Groups & Accommodation

How big are the groups?

Most departures run between 6 and 16 travelers — small enough to stay sociable and personal, large enough to keep prices low.

Is accommodation private or shared?

Our listed prices are based on shared twin or dorm-style rooms. If you'd prefer a private room, let us know when booking — it's available as a paid upgrade, subject to availability.

What's the typical traveler on these trips like?

A mix of solo travelers, couples, and friend groups, mostly in their 20s to 40s, looking for an affordable, sociable way to see Morocco.

Practical

Packing & Logistics

What should I pack?

Layers — Morocco's temperature swings a lot between day and night, especially in the desert and mountains. Bring sun protection, comfortable walking shoes, and a warm layer for desert camp nights.

Do I need travel insurance?

Yes, travel insurance is required for all our trips and is not included in the trip price.

Where do trips start and end?

Pickup and drop-off points vary by route — check the specific tour page for details, or ask us if you need a different city.

Send us a message and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
