About This Trip

Our cheapest and most local trip

Our cheapest and most local day trip. Bhalil is known for its troglodyte cave houses, some still inhabited today, while Sefrou is a relaxed former capital famous for cherry orchards, pottery, and a walled medina that feels like a smaller, quieter Fes.

At roughly 3.5 hours round trip, this is a great budget add-on alongside a longer day trip or a few days exploring the Fes medina — it wraps up around lunchtime.

FAQs

Sefrou & Bhalil Questions

Are the Bhalil cave houses still lived in?

Some are — a few families still occupy homes built into the hillside, and guided visits are respectful, small-group affairs rather than intrusive tourism.

Is this trip good for a short stay in Fes?

Yes — at roughly 3.5 hours round trip, it's our shortest full excursion, ideal if you only have a day or two in Fes and want a taste of the wider region.

Can I buy pottery in Sefrou?

Yes, Sefrou has a small pottery-making tradition, and the guided stop usually includes a chance to see (and buy from) a local workshop.
