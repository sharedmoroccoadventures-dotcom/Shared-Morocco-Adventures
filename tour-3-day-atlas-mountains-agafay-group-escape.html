Trip Overview

A short, sociable escape from the city

This is our shortest route — ideal if you only have a long weekend, or want a taster before committing to a longer desert trip. In three days you'll trade Marrakech's medina for mountain air in the High Atlas foothills, then swap peaks for dunes in the Agafay stone desert, all with a small group of fellow travelers.

Total driving distance is around 195 km, kept deliberately short so most of your time is spent walking, exploring, or relaxing rather than sitting in a minivan.

Round-trip from MarrakechMax 16 travelersDeparts every Friday
Day by Day

The journey, day by day

Day1
Day 1 · Mountains≈65 km · about 1.5 hrs driving

Marrakech to the High Atlas foothills

Morning group pickup in Marrakech and a short drive up into the High Atlas foothills near Imlil. Guided walk through terraced Berber villages and walnut groves, with a stop for mint tea with a local family. Overnight in a simple mountain guesthouse.

Day2
Day 2 · Desert≈95 km · about 2 hrs driving

Atlas foothills to the Agafay stone desert

Descend from the mountains and cross to the Agafay stone desert, a rocky plateau with sweeping High Atlas views. Afternoon camel ride across the plateau, followed by mint tea and a shared dinner at the desert camp as the sun sets over the dunes.

Day3
Day 3 · Return≈35 km · about 45 min driving

Agafay desert → back to Marrakech

Optional sunrise walk over the plateau before breakfast at camp. Short drive back to Marrakech, arriving by late morning with the rest of the day free. Trip ends.

Where You'll Stay

Accommodation on this route

Two nights, shared throughout to keep the price down. Rooms are simple and clean rather than luxurious — expect the same style and price bracket as these real budget properties in each location.

🏔️

Night 1 · Atlas foothills

A family-run Berber guesthouse in the Imlil valley, similar in style and price to trekking lodges used by budget operators in the area — shared twin rooms, home-cooked tagine dinner, mountain views from the terrace.

⛺

Night 2 · Agafay Desert

A shared Berber-style desert camp on the Agafay plateau, in the same bracket as dorm-tent camps like Selina Agafay Nomad Camp — simple bedded tents, shared bathrooms, campfire dinner under the stars.

🛏️

Private upgrade

Want your own room or tent instead of sharing? Let us know when booking — private accommodation is available on both nights as a paid upgrade, subject to availability.

FAQs

3-Day Atlas & Agafay Questions

How much hiking is involved?

The Atlas valley walk is a gentle 2–3 hour route on village paths, suitable for most fitness levels. No technical trekking gear is needed.

Is this different from the Agafay Desert day trip?

Yes — this 3-day route adds an overnight in the Atlas foothills and an overnight desert camp stay, rather than a single afternoon visit. It's a fuller version of our Agafay Desert day trip.

What should I pack?

Layers for cool mountain and desert-night temperatures, comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, and a scarf or hat for sun and wind.

Can solo travelers join?

Yes, most travelers on this route join solo. You'll be paired with a same-gender roommate for shared rooms unless you book a private upgrade.

What if the group doesn't fill up?

Departures run on fixed weekly dates regardless of group size — your trip runs even if only a few seats are booked.
