About Us

Morocco is better shared

We started Shared Morocco Adventures because private tours were pricing budget travelers out of an incredible country.

Our Story

Built for travelers, not just tourists

Morocco's Sahara, mountains, and medinas have long been sold at private-tour prices — a dedicated car and guide for every traveler. Great if you can afford it, but it locks a lot of people out.

Shared Morocco Adventures runs the same iconic routes on a shared model instead: one vehicle, one guide, one desert camp — split across a small group. The result is a trip that costs a fraction of the private price, and comes with a group of fellow travelers along for the ride.

We keep groups small (never more than 16), departures frequent and fixed, and the itineraries focused on what backpackers actually want to see.

What We Stand For

Our approach to group travel

🎒

Budget-first, not budget-cut

Lower cost comes from sharing, not from skipping the highlights. Every route hits the same landmarks a private tour would.

🧭

Fixed departures, always

Our trips run on schedule whether the group is 4 people or 16 — you never have to wait for a minimum headcount.

🌍

Local guides and drivers

Every group travels with a Morocco-based driver-guide who knows the routes, the history, and the best tea stops.
