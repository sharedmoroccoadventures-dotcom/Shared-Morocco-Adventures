Experience

Ten ways Morocco moves you

From camel treks to street-food nights, these are the moments our routes are built around.

01

The Sahara

Sunset over Erg Chebbi, silence, stars. The trip everyone talks about.

 02

Camel treks

Ride out into the dunes in a line of friends, just like the caravans did.

 03

Desert camps

Berber tents, a campfire, live drums and a sky full of stars.

 04

Sandboarding

Throw yourself down the slopes of Erg Chebbi. Ask about boards at the desert camp.

 05

Mountains

Atlas passes, valley walks and Toubkal, North Africa’s highest peak.

 06

Berber villages

Terraced valleys, argan co-ops and mint tea with local families.

 07

Street food

Tagines, msemen, snail soup if you dare. Eating together is half the trip.

 08

Medinas

Lantern-lit souks, spice pyramids and alleys made for getting lost.

 09

Waterfalls

Ouzoud’s 110 m falls, rainbows and the local Barbary macaques.

 10

Shared travel

Most travelers arrive solo and leave with a group chat full of friends.

01

Real budget pricing

We run the same iconic routes as private operators, but you share the vehicle, the guide and the cost with a small group. Trips cost a fraction of a private, chauffeur-driven itinerary without cutting the highlights.

One minivan, one guide, one camp

…split across up to 16 travelers.

02

Travel with your people

Small groups of solo travelers, couples and friends. Most of our guests arrive alone and leave with a group chat full of new friends.

03

Fixed weekly departures

No waiting around for a minimum group size. Our routes depart on set days every week, rain or shine, even if only a few seats are booked.
