6-Day Marrakech, Sahara Desert & Fes Tour

Marrakech → Ait Benhaddou → Dades Gorges → Merzouga Desert Camp → Fes

Trip Overview

Cross the Sahara and finish in Fes

Our most-booked Sahara route, extended one-way into Fes instead of looping back to Marrakech: airport pick-up and a night in Marrakech, the High Atlas passes and Berber valleys, a camel trek and overnight desert camp at Merzouga, then a scenic drive north to two nights in Fes with a full day of guided medina sightseeing.

Transport, driver-guide, and accommodation are shared across the group, which keeps the per-person cost far below a private itinerary covering the same route.

📍 One-way: Marrakech to Fes🚗 ≈920 km total👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Departs every Saturday

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Arrive

Arrival in Marrakech

≈6 km · about 15–20 min. Pick-up from Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and transfer to your hostel in the medina. The rest of the day is free to explore Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the surrounding souks — this is also your chance to meet the rest of the group before departure the next morning.

Day 2Dades

Marrakech to Dades Gorges

≈210 km · about 3.5 hrs driving. Early morning group departure from the hostel. Cross the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, stop at the Ait Benhaddou kasbah (a UNESCO World Heritage site), and continue through the Valley of Roses to an overnight stay in the Dades Gorges.

Day 3Desert

Dades Gorges to Merzouga Desert

≈235 km · about 4.5 hrs driving. Drive on via Tinghir and Erfoud to Merzouga, gateway to the Sahara. Late afternoon camel ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes, arriving at your desert camp in time for sunset — dinner around the fire, live music, and a night under the stars.

Day 4Fes

Merzouga to Fes

≈460 km · about 7–7.5 hrs driving. Optional sunrise over the dunes before camels carry the group back to Merzouga. Full day's drive north via Er-Rachidia, Midelt and the Middle Atlas cedar forests to Fes. Overnight in Riad Fez Mahal or La Perle de la Medina.

Day 5Fes

Fes sightseeing with a local guide

Full guided day through the Fes el-Bali medina — the Chouara tanneries, the souks, and the Bou Inania Madrasa. Overnight in the same Fes riad.

Day 6Depart

Transfer to Fes Airport

≈15 km · about 20–25 min. Free morning depending on flight time, then transfer from your Fes riad to Fes-Saïss Airport (FEZ) for departure. Trip ends.

Where You'll Stay

Accommodation on this route

Five nights, shared throughout.

🕍

Night 1 · Marrakech

Riad Dia, immediately next to Jemaa el-Fnaa — 9.3/10 on Hostelworld, rooftop pool, free breakfast and WiFi. Backup: Hostel Riad Marrakech Rouge, 2 minutes from the square.

🏰

Night 2 · Dades Gorges

Luxury Dades Riad or Auberge Tissadrin, half board (HB) — a kasbah-style riad in the Dades Valley with mudbrick rooms, terrace gorge views, and dinner included.

⛺

Night 3 · Merzouga desert camp

Ideal Desert Camp, half board (HB) — a Berber-style camp at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes with dinner and breakfast included, campfire music, and a night under the stars.

🏨

Nights 4–5 · Fes

Riad Fez Mahal or La Perle de la Medina — traditional medina riads with courtyard rooms, a short walk from the Fes el-Bali sights.

FAQs

6-Day Marrakech, Sahara & Fes Questions

Is this trip one-way, or does it return to Marrakech?

One-way — you're picked up in Marrakech on Day 1 and dropped off in Fes on Day 6, so there's no long drive back across the Atlas at the end.

Is accommodation shared?

Yes — the €412 price is based on shared twin/dorm rooms in Marrakech, the Dades Gorges and Fes, and a shared tent at the desert camp. A private room upgrade is available on request for an additional cost.

What should I pack for the desert night?

Desert nights get cold even in summer — bring a warm layer, plus sunscreen, a scarf or hat for wind and sun, and comfortable walking shoes.

Is the Fes sightseeing day guided?

Yes — Day 5 includes a full day with a local guide through the Fes el-Bali medina, covering the tanneries, souks and the Bou Inania Madrasa.

Can I extend this into Chefchaouen and the Middle Atlas?

Yes — see our 8-Day Marrakech, Sahara, Fes & Chefchaouen Tour, which adds a Chefchaouen day trip and a Middle Atlas/Ifrane day trip from Fes before your final departure.

What if the group doesn't fill up?

Departures run on fixed weekly dates regardless of group size — your trip runs even if only a few seats are booked.
