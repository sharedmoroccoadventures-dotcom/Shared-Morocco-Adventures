5-Day Marrakech, Atlas & Sahara Adventure

Marrakech → Ait Benhaddou → Dades Valley → Todra Gorge → Merzouga Desert Camp → Marrakech

Trip Overview

The Sahara route, at a gentler pace

Our 4-day Sahara tour condensed into a tight loop — this 5-day version spreads the same route over an extra day, giving you a full night in the Dades Valley and more time in the Berber villages along the Valley of Roses, instead of covering it all in one long drive.

Total driving distance is around 1,005 km round trip, shared with a small group the whole way.

📍 Round-trip from Marrakech👥 Max 16 travelers🗓 Departs every Tuesday

Day by Day

Itinerary

Day 1Depart

Marrakech to Skoura via Ait Benhaddou

≈210 km · about 3.5 hrs driving. Cross the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, stopping at the UNESCO-listed Ait Benhaddou kasbah, before continuing to the palm oasis of Skoura for the night.

Day 2Valleys

Skoura to Dades Gorge via the Valley of Roses

≈120 km · about 2 hrs driving. Drive through the Valley of Roses, with a stop at a local cooperative, then on to the dramatic rock formations of the Dades Gorge for an evening walk and overnight stay.

Day 3Desert

Dades Gorge to Merzouga via Todra Gorge

≈235 km total · about 4.5 hrs driving. Morning hike into the towering Todra Gorge (≈55 km/1 hr from Dades), then continue across the desert plains to Merzouga (≈180 km/3.5 hrs). Camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes for a night at the shared desert camp.

Day 4Return

Sahara camp to Ouarzazate

≈330 km · about 5 hrs driving. Optional sunrise over the dunes, then camels back to Merzouga and a scenic drive back across the Atlas via Tinghir, arriving in Ouarzazate for the night.

Day 5Arrive

Ouarzazate back to Marrakech

≈210 km · about 3.5 hrs driving. Final crossing of the High Atlas, arriving back in Marrakech by mid-afternoon. Trip ends.

Where You'll Stay

Accommodation on this route

Four nights, shared throughout. Expect the same style and price bracket as these real budget properties in each location.

🏰

Nights 1–2 · Skoura & Dades

Night 1 in Skoura is a simple kasbah-style guesthouse; Night 2 in the Dades Gorges is Luxury Dades Riad or Auberge Tissadrin, half board (HB) — mudbrick rooms, terrace views, dinner included.

⛺

Night 3 · Merzouga desert camp

Ideal Desert Camp, half board (HB) — a Berber-style camp at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes with dinner and breakfast included, campfire music, and a night under the stars.

🏨

Night 4 · Ouarzazate

A budget guesthouse near the medina, similar in style and price to Le Petit Riad — clean, simple twin rooms and a rooftop terrace.

FAQs

5-Day Sahara Adventure Questions

How is this different from the 4-Day Sahara tour?

Same core route, but spread over an extra day — you get a full night in the Dades Valley and more time in the Valley of Roses, rather than driving straight through.

Is accommodation shared?

Yes — the €329 price is based on shared twin or dorm-style rooms throughout. A private room upgrade is available on request for an additional cost.

What should I pack for the desert night?

Desert nights get cold even in summer — bring a warm layer, plus sunscreen, a scarf or hat for wind and sun, and comfortable walking shoes.

Can I join this trip as a solo traveler?

Absolutely — most travelers on this route arrive solo. You'll be paired with a same-gender roommate for shared rooms unless you book a private upgrade.

What if the group doesn't fill up?

Departures run on fixed weekly dates regardless of group size — your trip runs even if only a few seats are booked.
